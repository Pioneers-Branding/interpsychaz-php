Learn More about TMS Therapy

TMS targets areas of the brain affected by depression to improve functioning

TMS uses non-invasive technology to improve electrical functioning of neurons. Approximately 50-60% of patients report significant relief of their depressive symptoms, whereas around 30-40% of people report complete resolution of their depression.

TMS Therapy is:

  • Virtually side effect free
  • Non-invasive
  • Medication free
  • Covered by most insurances & Medicare
  • FDA approved
